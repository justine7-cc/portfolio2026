The Insight

Physical objects in an AI-generated world

In an era of AI-generated everything, creators crave tactile, physical objects that signal identity. Music has been dematerialized. The cassette figure brings it back: a collectible key to a digital world.

Warm Gold expression · Flora AI render
The Mechanic

Tap to play

Tap the figurine to your phone and it unlocks a digital experience: a playlist, an artist profile, exclusive content. Analog form, digital soul. No app required.

The Process

Built end to end with generative AI

  • 01 Consumer insight. Creators want to stand out in an AI-saturated landscape; music fans collect artifacts, but nothing modern exists.
  • 02 Concept and form. The cassette: universally recognized, nostalgic, compact. The robot face makes it collectible, not just functional.
  • 03 3D modeling and AI render. Built the clay model from scratch, then used Flora AI to apply materials, compressing weeks of 3D work into hours.
  • 04 Animation. Produced the tap-to-play product animation, visualizing the moment of digital unlocking.
