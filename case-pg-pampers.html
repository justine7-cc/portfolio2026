The Context

End-to-end owner of a $100M brand

Pampers is P&G Taiwan's #1 brand by market share with $100M in annual sales. As Senior Brand Manager in the Baby Care division, I owned P&L, user growth, creator programs, and integrated brand strategy.

Pampers No.1 campaign event with the brand team, Taipei
The Repositioning

From features to identity

Research showed that premium buyers were not comparing absorbency specs. They were making a statement about the kind of parent they are. I repositioned the brand from product-led to identity-driven storytelling and rebuilt creator briefs around that driver, delivering CTR 30% above benchmark.

Media & Commerce

A $2.5M+ digital P&L

I owned the annual digital media budget across Meta, YouTube, Google Search, DV360, LINE, Shopee, momo, and Yahoo.

  • Drove Double 11 to 140% YoY sales growth
  • Achieved 35x ROAS on the LINE CRM channel
CRM

97K to 300K+ members

I rebuilt the CRM platform into a full engagement ecosystem using customer segmentation and behavioral analytics, tripling membership from 97K to 300K+ and lifting conversion 40%.

The Luxury Launch

Taiwan’s first luxury influencer event in the category

I designed Taiwan's first luxury influencer launch event in the mother-and-baby category, owning KOL selection, persona-to-audience matching, and content briefing. 27 of 30 influencer groups amplified organically, delivering 3x projected impressions.
