The Context

Keeping music human

Spun Labs is a pre-seed generative AI startup (a16z Speedrun SR007) democratizing studio-quality sound for 100M+ independent creators. I contributed as an independent research contributor as part of my Stanford coursework and independent exploration.

spun.music: "Musicians, you already run your own business. Get treated like one."
The Problem

Creators are locked out of their own sound

  • A home studio costs $20K+; renting professional studios runs $100 to $200 per hour
  • Over 100 million independent artists make music without access to professional production tools
  • The fundamental recording stack has been largely unchanged for a century
The Research

What 44 creators told us

I ran qualitative interviews and a 44-person survey on AI adoption among independent creators. Two numbers defined the strategy:

  • 64% expressed concern about AI tools
  • 75% would adopt if creative control were preserved

The takeaway: the challenge is positioning and trust, not product capability. Creators do not fear AI; they fear losing authorship.

What I Built

From insight to GTM framework

  • Sized the $27.4B TAM across hardware replacement, subscription software, and creator tool adjacencies
  • Built audience personas and an adoption-barrier framework
  • Developed the positioning and messaging framework for a B2C mobile launch, including the IS / IS NOT brand definition
  • Prototyped MVP concepts in Lovable to validate ideas without engineering resources
The positioning during my involvement (Feb to May 2026): keep music human, a million dollar studio in your pocket, built for artists by artists
Outcomes

Research that made it into the deck

My research and market sizing fed into the team’s investor materials, which were presented to Oxford Seed Fund and a16z. The team went on to be selected for a16z Speedrun SR007 with $1M in funding.
